## English

- **App name**: `bearbell - Bear Bell`
- **Subtitle**: `A bear bell for every step`
- **Short description** (Play): `A bear bell that rings with every step, even in your pocket with the screen off.`
- **Promotional text**: `Let wildlife know you're coming. bearbell rings with every step on the trail, and you can turn it on or off right from the Lock Screen.`
- **Keywords**: `bear bell,hiking,trail,bell,bear,wildlife,trekking,camping,safety,outdoor,walking,forest`
- **Description**:

```
bearbell turns your phone into a bear bell.
Keep it in your pocket or bag and it rings with every step, letting wildlife know a person is nearby.

- Works with the screen off
It detects shaking and rings even when your phone is locked and in your pocket.

- Adjustable sensitivity
Choose High, Medium or Low to match your stride and where you carry your phone.

- Turn it on or off from the Lock Screen
On iPhone, use the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. On Android, use the notification. No need to open the app when you need quiet.

- An original bell sound
A clear, brass-like bell tone synthesized just for bearbell.

- No data collected
No accounts, ads or analytics. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Note: bearbell is an aid that may help reduce wildlife encounters. It does not guarantee your safety. Always follow local guidance and safety rules on the trail.
Note: Keeping bearbell on uses additional battery.
```

## 심사 메모 (App Review / Play 포그라운드 서비스 신고)

**App Store — Notes for Review (영어)**

```
bearbell is a bear bell for hikers. Its core feature is ringing a bell sound in the background while the phone is locked in a pocket, triggered by walking motion (accelerometer).
To do this, while the user has turned bearbell on, the app keeps an audio session active (UIBackgroundModes: audio) and plays the bell sound whenever shaking is detected. When the user turns it off, the audio session is released and the app no longer runs in the background.
How to test: tap "Turn on", lock the phone, and shake it — the bell rings. The Live Activity on the Lock Screen can turn it off and on.
No account is required. No data is collected.
```

**Google Play — 포그라운드 서비스 신고 (mediaPlayback)**

```
The foreground service plays the bear bell sound, which is the app's core feature: while the user has turned bearbell on, it listens to the accelerometer and plays the bell sound each time walking motion is detected, including while the screen is off and the phone is in a pocket. The user starts and stops it explicitly (in the app or from the notification). The service does not run when bearbell is off.
```
